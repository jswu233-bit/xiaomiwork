## 第1页｜Phase 1：试点结果得到持续验证

## Slide 1 | Phase 1: Sustained Validation of Pilot Results

![](<0814 Pilot Project Report to Era-Store_Efficiency_Project_Fix_01.png>)

1.\*\*先看转化结果：\*\*7 月月度 CR 达到 5.38%，环比提升 24%。从趋势看，项目启动后 CR 持续稳定增长，并始终高于项目开始前的基线，W32 达到最高水平。反观项目开始前，仅有 5 周能够达到目标，说明项目改善是有效的。
First, conversion results: The monthly CR reached 5.38% in July, up 24% MoM. After the project started, CR showed stable growth and remained above the previous baseline, reaching its highest level in W32. Before the project, only five weeks achieved the target. This proves that the improvement actions were effective.



2.\*\*再看销售结果：\*\*7 月 GMV 达到约 1.8B IDR，达成率 116%，比其他存量店平均水平高 66%。项目开始前，这一领先幅度平均为 45%，项目后进一步扩大了 21 个百分点。

进入 8 月后，门店依然保持强劲表现。截至今天早上的数据，GMV 已达 1.1B IDR，达成率明显领先时间进度，全国排名第二；同时，CR 也在 7 月基础上继续提升。

Then, sales results: July GMV reached around IDR 1.8B, achieving 116% of the target. This was 66% higher than the average of other existing stores. Before the project, the average lead was only 45%, so the project created an additional 21 percentage points of improvement.

The strong performance has continued into August. Based on this morning’s data, GMV has reached IDR 1.1B, with achievement clearly ahead of time progress and ranking No. 2 nationwide. CR has also continued to improve compared with July.



3.\*\*结论：\*\*提升 CR 不是最终目的，而是帮助门店获得更多订单、提升 GMV 的关键手段。项目最终证明，持续改善门店转化，可以带来更好的销售结果。

Conclusion: Improving CR is not the final goal. It is a key method to generate more orders and increase GMV. The project proves that continuously improving store conversion can lead to stronger sales results.

## 第4页｜Phase 3 准备：验证街边店模型

## Slide 4 | Phase 3 Prep: Validate the Street-Store Model

![](<0814 Pilot Project Report to Era-image.png>)

接下来，第三阶段我们准备重点聚焦街边店。
&#x20;8 月，我们会在第二阶段中对相关方法进行初步验证；到 9 月，再正式启动第三阶段，并扩大落地。

Next, Phase 3 will focus on street stores.
&#x20;In August, we will conduct an initial test of the related methods during Phase 2. Then, in September, we will officially launch Phase 3 and expand the implementation.



街边店和 Mall 店最大的区别，其实是在流量端。
&#x20;Mall 店有商场提供相对稳定的自然客流，而街边店缺少这样的流量基础，客流更少、波动也更大，门店需要更主动地寻找和吸引顾客。

所以，在“过店—进店—成交”这条链路中，第三阶段会从第一步开始干预，也就是优先解决流量问题。至于提升进店率和转化率，则可以继续沿用 Mall 店已经验证有效的改善方法。

The biggest difference between street stores and mall stores is traffic.
&#x20;Mall stores have relatively stable natural traffic from the shopping mall. Street stores do not have the same traffic base, so their traffic is usually lower and less stable. They need to find and attract customers more actively.

Therefore, in the “passing-by traffic–store entry–conversion” journey, Phase 3 will start from the first step and focus on improving traffic. For entry rate and conversion rate, we can continue using the methods already proven effective in mall stores.



第二阶段也会为第三阶段积累三个关键基础：有效的方法、具备改善能力的人，以及 MKT 的额外资源投入。
&#x20;有了这三个条件，我们才能把已经验证的方法更好地复制到街边店，并进一步放大项目效果。

Phase 2 will also provide three key foundations for Phase 3: proven methods, capable people, and additional marketing support.
&#x20;With these three elements, we can better apply the proven methods to street stores and further scale up the project results.
